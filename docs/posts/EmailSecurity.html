Email Security

DMARC is a policy instruction to prevent a email domain from being used for spoofing or phishing. I’ve never heard of this concept until I was assigned to review DMARC aggregate report for one of our customers. We will be locking it down soon once all reports have been reviewed, so I guess I better understand how it actually works. To me, it sounds nonsensical to lock down the domain we are configuring, rather than the domains sending the spoofing emails. I’m using this post to track my notes on what it is and how it works.
